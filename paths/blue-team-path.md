# Blue Team Learning Path

A path for defensive security, SOC operations, incident response, and digital forensics.

Understanding both sides of the security equation makes you a more effective practitioner — whether you end up on offense or defense.

---

## Prerequisites

- Basic networking knowledge (TCP/IP, DNS, HTTP)
- Linux and Windows command-line familiarity
- Understanding of common attack techniques (see [01 — Fundamentals](../01-fundamentals/))

---

## Phase 1: Security Foundations (Weeks 1-8)

| Step | Book | Time | What You'll Learn |
|------|------|------|-------------------|
| 1 | [The Basics of Hacking and Penetration Testing](../notes/basics-of-hacking-pentest.md) | 1-2 weeks | Understand what attackers do (know your enemy) |
| 2 | [Hacking: The Art of Exploitation](../notes/hacking-the-art-of-exploitation.md) | 3-4 weeks | Deep understanding of exploitation mechanics |
| 3 | CISSP For Dummies | 2 weeks | Security management framework |

---

## Phase 2: Defensive Framework (Weeks 9-14)

| Step | Book | Time | What You'll Learn |
|------|------|------|-------------------|
| 4 | [MITRE ATT&CK Reference](../notes/mitre-attacks.md) | 2 weeks | The language of adversary behavior |
| 5 | [Blue Team Cheat Sheet](../notes/blue-team-cheat-sheet.md) | 1-2 weeks | Defensive command reference |
| 6 | 100 Security Operation Center Tools | 1-2 weeks | SOC tool landscape |

**Lab work:** Set up a SIEM (Wazuh, ELK Stack). Practice log analysis.

---

## Phase 3: Detection & Response (Weeks 15-22)

| Step | Book | Time | What You'll Learn |
|------|------|------|-------------------|
| 7 | Common Sense Guide to Mitigating Insider Threats | 1-2 weeks | Insider threat awareness |
| 8 | Cybersecurity Strategies and Best Practices | 2 weeks | Organizational security strategy |
| 9 | Complete Guide to CISM Certification | 3-4 weeks | Information security management |

**Lab work:** Write detection rules. Practice with Sigma rules and YARA.

---

## Phase 4: Forensics (Weeks 23-30)

| Step | Book | Time | What You'll Learn |
|------|------|------|-------------------|
| 10 | [CHFI v01: Forensics Investigation Procedures](../notes/chfi-v01-forensics.md) | 3 weeks | Forensic methodology, evidence handling |
| 11 | CHFI v03: Investigating Data & Image Files | 2-3 weeks | File analysis, data recovery |

**Lab work:** Practice with Autopsy, Volatility. Analyze forensic images from forensics CTFs.

---

## Phase 5: Advanced Defense (Weeks 31-40)

| Step | Book | Time | What You'll Learn |
|------|------|------|-------------------|
| 12 | [Hacking Exposed: Network Security](../notes/hacking-exposed-network-security.md) | 3 weeks | Know the attack vectors to defend against |
| 13 | AI Tools in Cybersecurity – 2025 Edition | 2 weeks | AI-powered defensive tools |
| 14 | [Nmap Cookbook](../notes/nmap-cookbook.md) | 1-2 weeks | Use scanning for defensive asset discovery |

---

## Certification Milestones

| After Phase | Consider |
|-------------|----------|
| Phase 1 | **CompTIA Security+** — Entry-level certification |
| Phase 3 | **CISM** — Information Security Management |
| Phase 4 | **CHFI** — Computer Hacking Forensic Investigator |
| Phase 5 | **CISSP** — Certified Information Systems Security Professional |
| Ongoing | **BTL1/BTL2** — Blue Team Level 1/2 |
