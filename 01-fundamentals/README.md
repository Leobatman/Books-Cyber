# 01 — Fundamentals

Security foundations, ethical hacking concepts, and professional certifications.

This category covers the building blocks of cybersecurity knowledge — from core concepts and terminology to certification-oriented study material. Start here if you're new to the field.

---

## Recommended Study Order

1. **The Basics of Hacking and Penetration Testing** — Quick, accessible overview of the entire pentest methodology.
2. **Hacking: The Art of Exploitation** — Deeper technical foundation. Take time with the programming chapters.
3. **CEH All-in-One Exam Guide** — Use as a reference guide for broad topic coverage.
4. **Ghost in the Wires** — Read for perspective and social engineering mindset.

---

## Books in This Category

### Essential

#### Hacking: The Art of Exploitation, 2nd Edition
- **Author:** Jon Erickson
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Priority:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Key Topics:** C programming, x86 assembly, buffer overflows, format strings, networking fundamentals, shellcode, cryptography
- **Why It Matters:** Builds a deep understanding of *how* exploitation works at the lowest level. Unlike most books that teach tools, this one teaches the underlying concepts.
- **[Study Notes](../notes/hacking-the-art-of-exploitation.md)**

#### The Basics of Hacking and Penetration Testing
- **Author:** Patrick Engebretson
- **Level:** Beginner
- **Priority:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Key Topics:** Recon, scanning, exploitation, maintaining access, reporting
- **Why It Matters:** The most concise introduction to the pentest methodology available. Excellent first book.
- **[Study Notes](../notes/basics-of-hacking-pentest.md)**
