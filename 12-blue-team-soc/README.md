# 12 — Blue Team & SOC

SOC operations, threat detection, MITRE ATT&CK framework, and defensive security tools.

Understanding the blue team perspective makes you a better red teamer. These resources cover how defenders detect, analyze, and respond to threats — knowledge that is valuable whether you're attacking or defending.
